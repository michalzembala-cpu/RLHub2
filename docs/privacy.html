Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Short version: NexPlay has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your stats stay in a folder on your PC. Nothing is uploaded anywhere unless you explicitly use a feature that talks to an outside service, and each of those is listed below.

Who this covers

This policy covers the NexPlay desktop application for Windows, the nexplayhub.netlify.app website, and NexAds Publisher — a private tool the author uses to upload his own promotional videos to social platforms. All three are made by one person, not a company.

What NexPlay stores, and where

Everything the app records about you is written to a folder on your own computer:

  • %LocalAppData%\RLHub2 — your profile name, MMR and rank history, match and session records, saved crosshairs and settings.

There is no account, no login and no sync. Deleting that folder deletes your data. Uninstalling the app leaves the folder in place so an update does not wipe your history; remove it by hand if you want it gone.

When the app talks to the internet

  • Public game stats. The app queries public profile and match data for the in-game name you enter, from the game publishers' public endpoints.
  • Updates. The app checks GitHub Releases for a newer installer. GitHub receives the request as any download would.
  • AI assistant (optional). If you use the built-in assistant, the text of your question is sent to the Groq API to produce an answer. Don't type anything into it you wouldn't send to a third-party service. Not using the assistant sends nothing.
  • Local network profile sharing (optional). If you switch it on, the app serves your profile over your own Wi-Fi on port 8777 so your phone can read it. This never leaves your network.

The app sends no telemetry, no analytics and no crash reports.

Screen reading

To capture your real MMR and rank, the app reads what is already drawn on your screen and looks for the numbers. Images are processed in memory and are not saved to disk or sent anywhere. The app does not read the game's memory or modify the game.

NexAds Publisher and social platforms

NexAds Publisher is not a product for other people — it is a script the author runs on his own computer to publish promotional videos for his own apps. When it is connected to a YouTube or TikTok account:

  • It requests only the permissions needed to upload a video and read the basic profile information the platform requires for that upload.
  • Access tokens are stored in a single file on the author's computer and are never shared.
  • It does not read, collect or store content belonging to any other user, and it does not post on anyone else's behalf.
  • Revoking access in your TikTok or Google account settings ends it immediately.

This website

nexplayhub.netlify.app sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The hosting provider (Netlify) keeps standard server logs, which include the IP address of anyone requesting a page.

Children

These tools are not directed at children under 13, and nothing here is collected knowingly from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions, or a request to delete something: open an issue at github.com/michalzembala-cpu/RLHub2/issues.
